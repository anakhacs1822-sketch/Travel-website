DISCOVER ALLEPPEY

The Venice of the East

Alleppey, officially known as Alappuzha, is one of Kerala's most beautiful destinations. It is famous for its peaceful backwaters, traditional houseboats and beautiful villages.

A journey through Alleppey's waterways lets you experience Kerala at a slower and more peaceful pace.
