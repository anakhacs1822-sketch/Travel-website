THE COASTAL SOUL OF KERALA

Varkala is one of Kerala's most distinctive coastal destinations, where dramatic laterite cliffs rise above the Arabian Sea.

Walk beside swaying coconut palms, watch the sunset from the cliff, explore peaceful beaches and experience the relaxed spirit of God's Own Country.
